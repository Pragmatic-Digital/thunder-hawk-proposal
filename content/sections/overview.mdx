[Client Name]'s requirements extend beyond a new website. The platform needs to support different customer groups, account relationships, pricing arrangements and delivery rules, while keeping [Core System] at the centre of the operation.

A headless architecture gives us the flexibility to build around those requirements rather than adapting [Client Name]'s processes to fit a more prescriptive ecommerce platform. Combined with a custom frontend, it gives us the foundation to deliver a modern customer experience while retaining control over the bespoke commerce and integration logic behind it.

The main priorities are to make products easier to find, give customers clearer access to stock, pricing and order information, and reduce routine administration for [Client Name]'s teams. The rebuild would also provide more flexible content management and a clearer website, with appropriate experiences for each customer audience.
