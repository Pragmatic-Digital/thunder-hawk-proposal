## Our experience with similar challenges

Two projects show how we've tackled the same challenges a new ThunderHawk site would need to
solve: bringing 3D to life in the browser, and delivering large volumes of visual media quickly and
reliably.

### Mulholland Media: fast browsing and timelapse generation for large image libraries

**The brief.** Mulholland Media runs a network of construction site cameras, capturing images throughout
the working day on projects lasting anything from a single day to several years. Its clients needed a
quicker, more intuitive way to review progress over time.

**What we built.** A new viewer, built as a React application and delivered through a WordPress plugin,
backed by cloud storage and automated processing that creates optimised versions of every image.

- **Images:** visitors land straight on the latest imagery and scrub through weeks or months on a
timeline. Preloading and progressive loading mean images appear without waiting.

- **Compare:** any two moments can be viewed side by side with a draggable divider, choosing the
date and time for each.

<Figure src="/images/screencapture-mulholland-pragmaticdigital-co-uk-construction-site-downtown-tower-2026-09-25-13_08_01.png" alt="Mulholland comparison tool for comparing images" title="Mulholland Viewer" aspect="2:1" /> 

- **Videos:** select any date range and generate a downloadable timelapse on demand.

<Figure src="/images/screencapture-mulholland-pragmaticdigital-co-uk-construction-site-downtown-tower-2026-09-25-13_08_34.png" alt="Mulholland video generation tool" title="Mulholland Viewer" aspect="4:3" /> 

**Why it's relevant.** During our audit, some video files on your current site failed to load after being
refused by the server. Mulholland's viewer was built around exactly this kind of problem: serving large
volumes of high quality media quickly and reliably. The same approach would let ThunderHawk's
renders and footage load consistently, and could power features such as comparing early renders with
the finished piece.

### Coryton: full website and interactive 3D facility

**The brief.** Coryton develops, formulates and blends speciality fuels. It needed a website that could
explain complex, technical operations clearly to a wide range of audiences.

**What we built.** We designed and developed the full site in WordPress, from scroll-driven video
transitions and animated interactions to The Lab, a content hub bringing together articles, research and
video with filtering and infinite scroll.
The centrepiece is an interactive facility map built in Three.js. Rather than relying on photography and
diagrams, visitors can explore the site in 3D and discover each area of the operation for themselves.

**Why it's relevant.** Your current site already uses 3D through Spline. A new site would need the same
balance we struck for Coryton: 3D that's engaging but loads and performs well across devices, motion
that guides rather than distracts, and a CMS your team can update easily.
