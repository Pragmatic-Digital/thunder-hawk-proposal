> Our recommendation is to proceed with a headless e-commerce rebuild.

We believe this provides the strongest fit for the requirements you have set out and the way your platform needs to operate in practice. It gives us the flexibility to build around your customer relationships, account-specific functionality and system integration requirements without forcing those into the constraints of a more prescriptive platform.

The current budget estimate for this approach is £100,000 + VAT, subject to final scope and technical validation.

Enhancement to your existing platform remains available if your priority is to make targeted improvements while making a significantly lower initial investment. However, this should be viewed as an investment in extending and improving the existing system rather than an alternative route to the same end result.

Other rebuild platforms are also viable options, but based on the requirements currently understood, we believe our recommended approach provides you with greater flexibility and long-term control for a platform with this level of bespoke customer and integration functionality.

## Confirming the approach

The next step would be to work through the proposal with your team and confirm the preferred route.

If you wish to proceed with the recommended rebuild approach, we would then move into final technical validation and project planning before development begins.

This would allow us to:

1. Confirm the detailed scope and assumptions behind the £100,000 budget estimate
2. Validate the more complex customer, pricing and system workflows
3. Confirm the technical architecture and required third-party services
4. Finalise the ongoing infrastructure and support costs
5. Agree the Phase 1 scope and any functionality to be introduced later
6. Confirm the delivery programme, milestones and target launch window
7. Agree the commercial structure and commencement date

Once these points are confirmed, we can produce the final delivery plan and move into the discovery and technical validation stage.

## A platform for the next stage of your growth

The objective of this project is not simply to replace your existing platform.

It is an opportunity to give your team and customers a platform that is easier to use, more efficient for the internal team to manage and better suited to the customer relationships and operational processes that have developed around your business.

Our existing relationship gives us a strong starting point. We already understand much of your current platform, its integration challenges and the areas where the existing service creates friction.

The recommended rebuild gives us the opportunity to combine that knowledge with a fresh customer experience and a technical foundation that can continue to evolve with your business over the long term.

We would welcome the opportunity to work through the proposal with you, answer any questions on the scope and budget, and agree the route forward.
