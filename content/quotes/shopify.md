## Overview

The project will deliver a new digital platform for Thunder Hawk, replacing the existing Magento trade portal with a more flexible, scalable and user-friendly solution built on Shopify Plus.

The new platform will support Verona’s growing range of customer groups, including independent retailers, housebuilders, contractors, merchants, key accounts and end users, while providing each audience with a tailored experience based on their account, catalogue, pricing, permissions and commercial requirements.

The existing Magento platform was originally designed primarily around independent retailers and is now increasingly difficult to adapt to Verona’s broader customer structure.

Shopify Plus will provide the core ecommerce and B2B foundation, including customer accounts, company and branch structures, customer-specific catalogues, pricing, payment terms and checkout functionality. Where Verona’s requirements extend beyond Shopify’s native B2B model, bespoke application functionality will be developed to support more complex relationships and workflows.

A key focus of the project will be improving product discovery and customer self-service. The new site will provide significantly improved search and filtering, richer product information, real-time stock visibility, customer-specific pricing and catalogues, order history, invoices, delivery tracking, downloadable resources and account management functionality.

The search functionality has been identified throughout Verona’s workshops as one of the highest-priority improvements to the existing platform.

The platform will integrate closely with Orderwise, which will remain the primary operational source for product, customer, pricing, stock, account and order information. A custom Shopify integration will synchronise the required data between Orderwise and Shopify, while orders placed through the new website will continue to be passed back into Orderwise for processing.

Shopify’s Companies and Company Locations functionality will be used to support standard customer, branch and account structures. Additional custom functionality will be developed where required to support Verona’s more complex housebuilder model, including contractors working across multiple housebuilders and developments, development-specific delivery addresses, customer-specific catalogues and pricing, and configurable commercial rules.

For customers such as Wickes, Selco, Jewson and Travis Perkins who may not place orders directly through the website, the platform will also provide a dedicated self-service portal. This will allow users to access relevant products, stock information, customer-specific pricing, order history, invoices, delivery tracking and resources, helping reduce reliance on Verona’s sales support team.

The public-facing website will be redesigned to better represent Thunder Hawk as a whole, introducing a stronger group-level presence and clearer navigation between its different divisions and digital properties. The new design will move away from the current darker Magento aesthetic and create a lighter, more contemporary and design-led experience, while maintaining a strong focus on high-value journeys such as login, search and new product discovery.

Shopify’s content management capabilities will also give Verona greater day-to-day control over the site. Flexible theme sections, metafields and structured content will allow internal teams to manage landing pages, promotional content, banners and other key areas without relying on developer input for routine changes.

The project will be delivered using a phased approach, with the core platform and highest-value customer journeys forming the initial release. More complex or lower-priority functionality can then be introduced in later phases. The commercial implications of that phasing, including the share of investment required in Phase 1, are set out in [Phasing](#phasing).

The overall objective is to create a single, modern digital ecosystem that improves the experience for Verona’s customers, reduces manual administration, increases internal control over content and commerce, and provides a scalable foundation for the continued growth of the business.

## Assumptions

This estimate assumes that Verona is willing to make appropriate use of Shopify Plus’ native B2B model, including Companies, Company Locations, Catalogues and standard checkout functionality.

Requirements that cannot be accommodated within Shopify’s native model, particularly the housebuilder/development structures, advanced Orderwise workflows and post-order amendments, will require custom application development and are included on an indicative basis pending technical discovery.
