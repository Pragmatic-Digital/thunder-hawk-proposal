## Overview

The project will deliver a new digital platform, replacing the existing system with a more flexible, scalable and user-friendly solution built on a managed SaaS commerce platform.

The new platform will support [Client Name]’s range of customer groups and use cases, providing each audience with a tailored experience based on their account, catalogue, pricing, permissions and commercial requirements.

The existing platform was originally designed around simpler use cases and is increasingly difficult to adapt to [Client Name]’s broader customer structure.

A managed SaaS platform will provide the core ecommerce and B2B foundation, including customer accounts, customer group structures, customer-specific catalogues, pricing and checkout functionality. Where [Client Name]’s requirements extend beyond the platform’s native model, custom application functionality will be developed to support more complex relationships and workflows.

A key focus of the project will be improving product discovery and customer self-service. The new platform will provide significantly improved search and filtering, richer product information, real-time data visibility, customer-specific pricing and catalogues, order history, invoices, delivery tracking, downloadable resources and account management functionality.

The platform will integrate closely with [Core System], which will remain the primary operational source for product, customer, pricing, stock, account and order information. A custom integration will synchronise the required data, while orders placed through the new website will be passed back into [Core System] for processing.

The SaaS platform’s native customer structures will be used to support standard account models. Additional custom functionality will be developed where required to support [Client Name]’s more complex customer relationships, including different customer types, delivery arrangements, customer-specific catalogues and pricing, and configurable commercial rules.

For customer groups that may not place orders directly through the website, the platform will also provide a dedicated self-service information portal. This will allow users to access relevant products, data, pricing, order history, invoices, delivery tracking and resources, helping reduce reliance on manual support.

The public-facing website will be redesigned to create a modern, contemporary and design-led experience, while maintaining a strong focus on high-value journeys such as login, search and new product discovery.

The SaaS platform’s content management capabilities will also give [Client Name] greater day-to-day control over the site. Flexible sections, metafields and structured content will allow internal teams to manage landing pages, promotional content, banners and other key areas without relying on developer input for routine changes.

The project will be delivered using a phased approach, with the core platform and highest-value customer journeys forming the initial release. More complex or lower-priority functionality can then be introduced in later phases. The commercial implications of that phasing, including the share of investment required in Phase 1, are set out in [Phasing](#phasing).

The overall objective is to create a modern digital ecosystem that improves the experience for [Client Name]’s customers, reduces manual administration, increases internal control over content and commerce, and provides a scalable foundation for continued growth.

## Assumptions

This estimate assumes that [Client Name] is willing to make appropriate use of the platform’s native B2B model, including customer structures, catalogues and standard checkout functionality.

Requirements that cannot be accommodated within the platform’s native model, particularly complex customer relationships and advanced [Core System] workflows, will require custom application development and are included on an indicative basis pending technical discovery.
